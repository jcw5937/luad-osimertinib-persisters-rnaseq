# Credits and citations

This is a portfolio re-analysis of public data. It is not affiliated with or
endorsed by the original authors, UCSF, the TCGA Research Network, or the NCI.

## Experiment and raw data

All experimental design, cell culture, osimertinib treatment, and RNA
sequencing were performed by the original authors (Bivona lab, UCSF). This
project re-analyzes their public data only.

> Haderk F, Chou Y-T, Cech L, et al. Focal adhesion kinase-YAP signaling axis
> drives drug-tolerant persister cells and residual disease in lung cancer.
> *Nature Communications* 15, 3741 (2024). doi:10.1038/s41467-024-47423-0

Raw sequencing data: NCBI GEO accession **GSE255958**.

## Patient data — TCGA

> The results shown here are in whole or part based upon data generated by the
> TCGA Research Network: https://www.cancer.gov/tcga.

TCGA-LUAD data were accessed via the NCI Genomic Data Commons (GDC).
